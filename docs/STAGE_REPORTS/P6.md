# Stage Report P6: City Console, Interactive Risk Map, Alerts & Punjab Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
Stage P6 implements the comprehensive, production-grade City Console UI, Interactive Geospatial Risk Map, Official Alerts Integration, and Punjab Regional Overview for NEXSTORM.

The implementation builds strictly on verified foundational infrastructure from P0–P5 without manufacturing synthetic production rainfall, fake incidents, or unvalidated flood depths:
- **Dual Spatial Resolution Support**: 250m pilot grid (1,998 cells) for Amritsar municipal area, and 50m micro-topography sub-grid (699 cells) for the GNDU campus.
- **Interactive Risk Map**: MapLibre/Leaflet rendering of GeoJSON polygons with dynamic risk classification styling, static susceptibility layer toggling, 15 verified historical hotspot markers (`hotspots.json`) with news source provenance links, and interactive cell selection with visual focus ring.
- **Operational Mode Switching**: Interactive switching between `live` (Open-Meteo ensemble with snapshot fallback), `replay` (`2025-09-01_amritsar` historical event), and `scenario` (deterministic hyetograph simulations with customizable intensity).
- **Rainfall Timeline & Risk Telemetry**: Interactive timeline scrubber with hourly hyetograph bars, median RTI, beyond-calibration warning threshold alerts ($RTI > 2.25$), and class distribution breakdown (Minimal, Low, Moderate, High, Severe).
- **Cell Inspection Engine**: Synchronous and asynchronous integration with `/api/cell/[cellId]` displaying topographic factor contributions ($f_{\text{builtUp}}, f_{\text{depression}}, f_{\text{flatness}}, f_{\text{lowPoint}}, f_{\text{density}}$), nearest verified incident within 500m, terrain overrides, and scientific limitations.
- **Official Statutory Alerts**: Dedicated NDMA SACHET Common Alerting Protocol (CAP) feed integration via `/api/official-alerts` with severity badges, source attribution, and statutory authority distinction.
- **Punjab Regional Overview**: Geographic boundary enforcement detailing Tier 1 (operational pilot: Amritsar & GNDU), Tier 2 (candidate cities: Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Patiala, Bathinda, Mohali), and Tier 3 (regional context), preventing false claims of statewide modeling.
- **Scientific Methodology & Guardrails**: Prominent permanent disclaimer, transparent formula documentation for RTI and Susceptibility, and 5 explicit non-claims.

## 5. Unmodeled Limitations & Scientific Guardrails
1. **Pilot Extent**: Only Amritsar municipal boundary and GNDU campus have verified topographic feature grids. Other Punjab districts (Ludhiana, Jalandhar, etc.) are unmodeled and intentionally excluded from risk scoring.
2. **Hydraulic Non-Claims**: NEXSTORM models relative pluvial waterlogging susceptibility driven by terrain indices and rainfall intensity. It does **not** simulate hydrodynamic pipe surcharge, stormwater network backflow, or building water ingress.
3. **Flood Depth Disclaimer**: Risk classes (Minimal, Low, Moderate, High, Severe) denote relative susceptibility rank, never inundation depth in centimeters.
4. **Statutory Non-Interference**: NEXSTORM model outputs are never labeled as official warnings or evacuation advisories. Official bulletins remain strictly attributed to IMD and NDMA SACHET.
