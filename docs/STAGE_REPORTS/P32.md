## 4. Honest Evaluation & Ground Truth Disclosure

1. **Perspective Shift (UAV Aerial vs. Smartphone Ground):**
   - Public research datasets like `FloodNet` and `FloodRescue-AI` train primarily on UAV/drone aerial nadir imagery ($200\text{--}500\text{ ft}$).
   - In Punjab, citizen and municipal reports are predominantly ground-level smartphone photos with horizontal perspective and obstruction.
   - **Enforced Guardrail:** Models trained exclusively on nadir aerial imagery are not treated as validated ground-level classifiers. Multimodal vision and deterministic rule-based classifiers are used for street-level images.
2. **Water Depth Calibration:**
   - Optical photos cannot measure exact millimeter water depth. Depth is categorized strictly as qualitative condition proxies (`curb_ponding`, `flowing_surface_water`, `deep_standing_water`, `submerged`) relative to visible physical reference objects.
3. **Sample Sizes in Verification Archive:**
   - Pre-seeded verified municipal incident archive: 3 historical cloudburst benchmarks (SSSS Chowk Underpass, GNDU Campus Gate, Civil Lines Railway Sump).
   - Synthetic & live field test inputs: 10 test vectors covering all severity grades and error modes.
4. **Satellite Latency Reality:**
   - Optical Sentinel-2 is 100% cloud-blind during active monsoon downpours.
   - C-SAR Sentinel-1 penetrates clouds but has a 12-day revisit latency, making it unusable for 1-hour early warning.
