## 1. Executive Summary

Stage P12 implements the second foundational pillar of the **NEXSTORM** urban waterlogging intelligence system: **building-level exposure assessment**.

NEXSTORM's three architectural pillars:
1. **Area-level waterlogging susceptibility and rainfall-triggered risk** (250m macro-catchment grid).
2. **Building-level exposure assessment** (individual footprint associations and macro-catchment spatial overlap).
3. **Road-level risk assessment & flood-aware routing foundation** (corridor risk aggregation, underpass recognition, and dynamic elevation).

P12 delivers a defensible, performance-conscious implementation of building exposure across Amritsar:
- **Comprehensive Footprint Inventory:** Utilizes 360,252 building footprints from Google Open Buildings V3 with metric areas in UTM Zone 43N (EPSG:32643), plus 17,961 hotspot-clustered footprints and 1,337 GNDU campus footprints.
- **Multi-Cell Spatial Intersection:** Evaluates building footprints spanning multiple 250m cells by calculating exact subsegment areas and proportions, computing area-weighted susceptibility ($\bar{s}$) and conservative maximum susceptibility ($s_{\max}$).
- **Strict Scientific Guardrails:** Clearly separates **area susceptibility** (macro-catchment terrain model), **building exposure** (spatial overlap of footprint with hazard cells), and **building vulnerability** (structural properties such as plinth height, basement presence, or construction material are strictly **not invented** or claimed).
- **Fast Viewport API:** `GET /api/buildings` supporting geographic bounding-box queries, cell ID queries, area filters, and pagination without sending the entire 360,000-building dataset to the browser.
- **Console Map & Detail Panel:** Interactive building footprint layer in `RiskMap.tsx` with exposure category coloring and click-to-inspect popups, paired with aggregated cell-level building summaries in `CellDetailPanel.tsx`.

## 3. Building Exposure Model Formulation

### 3.1 Multi-Cell Spatial Intersections
Buildings in dense urban environments often sit across the boundaries of 250m grid cells. P12 rejects centroid-only snapping for multi-cell buildings:
- **Footprint Metric Area:**
  $$A_{\text{total}} = \sum_{c \in C_b} A_c + A_{\text{unmatched}}$$
- **Proportional Share:**
  $$\text{fraction}_c = \frac{A_c}{A_{\text{total}}}$$
- **Area-Weighted Susceptibility:**
  $$\bar{s}_b = \sum_{c \in C_b} s_c \cdot \text{fraction}_c$$
- **Conservative Maximum Susceptibility:**
  $$s_{\max, b} = \max_{c \in C_b} s_c$$
- **Unmatched Fraction:**
  $$\text{unmatchedFraction}_b = \max\left(0, 1.0 - \sum_{c \in C_b} \text{fraction}_c\right)$$

### 3.2 Exposure Classification
- **High Catchment Exposure (`high_catchment`):** $s_{\max} \ge 0.66$.
- **Moderate Catchment Exposure (`moderate_catchment`):** $0.33 \le s_{\max} < 0.66$.
- **Minimal / Low Catchment Exposure (`low_catchment` / `minimal`):** $s_{\max} < 0.33$.
- **Unmatched (`unmatched`):** Footprint extends beyond municipal grid boundary ($\ge 99\%$ unmatched).

### 3.3 Separation of Exposure and Vulnerability
- **Susceptibility:** Reflects surrounding 250m terrain slope, elevation depression, and built-up land cover.
- **Exposure:** Reflects physical footprint co-location with higher-susceptibility runoff catchments.
- **Vulnerability:** Structural resistance (plinth height, basement presence, wall materials, floor count). **Omitted / Unclaimed.** NEXSTORM makes no assumptions about building structural integrity or flood proofing.
- **Scientific Caveat:** Attached to every building record:
  > *"Macro-catchment flood exposure only. Does not predict indoor water depth, structural damage, or plinth clearance."*

## 4. API & Visualization Integration

1. **Building Exposure Endpoint (`GET /api/buildings`):**
   - Supports `bbox=minLng,minLat,maxLng,maxLat` and `cellId=A-XXXX`.
   - Supports `exposureCategory` filtering (`high_catchment`, `moderate_catchment`, `low_catchment`).
   - Supports `minAreaM2` and `maxAreaM2` filtering.
   - Paginates with `limit` (max 500) and `offset`.
   - Returns 404 for non-Amritsar queries.
2. **Interactive Map Layer (`RiskMap.tsx`):**
   - Toggleable **Building Footprints** layer with color-coded polygons:
     - Red (`#ef4444`): High Catchment Exposure
     - Amber (`#f59e0b`): Moderate Catchment Exposure
     - Blue (`#3b82f6`): Minimal / Low Exposure
     - Slate (`#94a3b8`): Unmatched Outside Grid
   - Click-to-inspect popup displaying Building ID, Confidence score, Metric Area ($m^2$), Primary Grid Cell, Susceptibility score, and scientific caveats.
3. **Cell Inspection Panel (`CellDetailPanel.tsx`):**
   - Renders building exposure statistics when inspecting any cell: Footprint Count, Total Footprint Area ($m^2$), Mean Footprint Area ($m^2$), and Catchment Exposure Tier.
