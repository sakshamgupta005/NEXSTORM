# Stage Report P7: Scientific Validation, Risk Calibration & Data Reliability

## 1. Executive Summary
Stage P7 performs a comprehensive scientific validation, risk calibration, and data reliability audit of NEXSTORM. In accordance with the governing directive, this stage **prioritized validating and stress-testing the existing model over adding speculative features**, strictly refusing to manufacture artificial data, fake incident records, or ungrounded flood depths.

### Key Audit Findings
1. **Zero Training Leakage in Model Parameters**: The model's static susceptibility weights and multi-window RTI thresholds were defined a priori from Copernicus DEM terrain and 15 seasons (2010–2024 JJAS) of ERA5-Land reanalysis. Historical incident locations were **never used to fit or optimize model parameters**.
2. **Empirical Hotspot Recovery ($N=15$)**: 8 of 15 validated hotspot cells ($53.33\%$) fall within the top 20% of susceptibility scores ($2.67\times$ lift over the 20% spatial null). The median hotspot cell percentile is **81.93%**. However, due to the small sample size ($N=15$), the exact 95% Clopper-Pearson binomial confidence interval is wide: $[26.59\%, 78.73\%]$.
3. **Severe News-Reporting Selection Bias**: A built-up density baseline achieves an apparent ROC-AUC of **0.8658**, compared to **0.5609** for the frozen terrain-informed susceptibility score. Investigation revealed this is an artifact of journalistic selection bias: all 15 reports originated from *The Tribune India* and documented traffic bottlenecks in prominent commercial arteries (Hall Gate, Putlighar, Lawrence Road). Unreported peripheral cells are unlabelled, not confirmed flood-free. Adopting a model driven solely by built-up density was scientifically rejected (`adopted: false`).
4. **High Sensitivity vs. False Alarm Burden**: The model's multi-window RTI rule ($\max(RTI) \ge 0.5$) detects **66.67% (6/9)** of historical reported event days, whereas the standard IMD 24h 64.5mm threshold captures **0.0% (0/9)**, confirming that 24-hour totals fail to capture short convective cloudbursts. However, the model rule triggers on **45.0 days per 122-day monsoon season (~36.9% of days)**, incurring a substantial false alarm burden that must be explicitly communicated to users.
5. **Spatial Resolution Limits**: The 250m city cells ($62,500\text{ m}^2$) indicate macro-catchment susceptibility and cannot support claims of street-level curb or doorway inundation.

### 3.2 Temporal Holdout & Leakage Audit
- **2023 Monsoon Batch** (5 locations: H-0001 to H-0005): 4 of 5 cells in top 20% susceptibility ($80.0\%$, Exact 95% CI: $[28.4\%, 99.5\%]$).
- **2024 Monsoon Batch** (10 locations: H-0006 to H-0015): 4 of 10 cells in top 20% susceptibility ($40.0\%$, Exact 95% CI: $[12.2\%, 73.8\%]$).
- **Temporal Consistency**: Model weights were fixed a priori and never tuned on either batch.

### 3.3 Historical Event Detection vs. Alert Burden
- **Reported Events Captured**: 6 of 9 calendar days ($\text{POD} = 66.67\%$, 95% Wilson CI: $[35.42\%, 87.94\%]$).
- **IMD 24h Baseline Captured**: 0 of 9 calendar days ($\text{POD} = 0.00\%$, 95% Wilson CI: $[0.00\%, 29.91\%]$).
- **Seasonal Alert Burden**: Model triggers on **45.0 days per 122-day monsoon season (~36.9% of days)**, whereas the IMD baseline triggers on 0.2 days/season.
- **Trade-off**: The multi-window RTI successfully detects sub-daily convective cloudbursts that evade 24-hour thresholds, but incurs high alert frequency.

## 4. Live Data Reliability & Provenance Safeguards

1. **Missing Data Imputation Warning**: If upstream Open-Meteo intervals contain `null` or `undefined` values, they are imputed as 0mm and explicitly recorded in `series.provenance.note` (`DATA QUALITY WARNING: N missing forecast interval(s) imputed as 0 mm`).
2. **Snapshot Clock-Skew Guard**: Snapshots with future `savedAt` timestamps ($>60\text{s}$) are rejected to protect against clock skew and file tampering.
3. **Deterministic Single-Series Fallback**: When ensemble spreads are unavailable (or in replay/deterministic fallback), `confidence` is truthfully set to `'unavailable'` rather than manufacturing synthetic spreads.
4. **Statutory Warning Distinction**: Official NDMA SACHET CAP feeds remain strictly separated from experimental model output.

## 6. Recommendations for Next Stage
Based on the empirical findings, the recommended next priorities are **data-grounding improvements**, not speculative feature expansion:
1. **Prospective Crowd / Gauge Calibration**: The primary bottleneck to tighter confidence intervals is the small sample size ($N=15$). Deploy a structured civic reporting protocol or municipal gauge telemetry feed to log confirmed waterlogging depth and duration prospectively.
2. **High-Resolution Doppler Radar Integration**: Coarse 10–25 km satellite reanalysis missed localized cloudbursts (e.g. 2023-09-02 East Mohan Nagar). Integrating IMD Doppler radar reflectivity ($Z-R$ relation) would drastically reduce missed sub-daily events.
3. **Alert Threshold Pruning**: Explore optimizing the lower RTI threshold from $0.5$ to $0.75$ to reduce the seasonal alert burden from 45 days/season to a more operationally viable rate without sacrificing major convective events.
