## 3. Critical Issues Discovered & Fixes Implemented

### 3.1 Hardened Moderator Authentication Token
- **Discovery**: `src/app/api/observations/[id]/verify/route.ts` permitted fallback development reviewer tokens (`dev-reviewer`, `test-moderator-token`) unconditionally and used a hardcoded fallback string when `OBSERVATION_MODERATOR_KEY` was unset.
- **Fix**: Updated verification authorization logic. In production environments (`NODE_ENV === 'production'`), requests strictly require a cryptographically secure `OBSERVATION_MODERATOR_KEY` configured via server environment variables. Development reviewer tokens are restricted to development and test environments. Added `OBSERVATION_MODERATOR_KEY=` to [.env.example](file:///c:/Users/rspma/Downloads/NEXSTORM/.env.example).

### 3.2 Reporter Privacy Enforcement
- Verified that `reporterHash` is consistently stripped from public API query responses in `GET /api/observations` and verification responses. IP addresses and user agents are salted with a local secret before hashing; plain text IPs are never persisted.

### 3.3 Unknown-State Semantics (No Silent Safety Assumptions)
- **Rule**: Missing, stale, or unmatched evidence must never silently default to a low-risk classification.
- In `RoadRiskService.evaluateCandidateRoutes()`, unmapped corridors are explicitly measured as `unknownRiskDistanceM` and `unknownRiskFraction`. High unknown fractions ($> 40\%$) trigger a mandatory `caution` recommendation, and an unknown-coverage penalty is added to the composite score.

### 3.4 Freshness Rules & Provenance Registry
- Implemented `DATASET_PROVENANCE_REGISTRY` in [src/lib/provenance.ts](file:///c:/Users/rspma/Downloads/NEXSTORM/src/lib/provenance.ts) defining source provenance, spatial resolution, freshness TTLs, and explicit primary limitations for all 5 core datasets.
- Implemented `evaluateObservationFreshness(observedAt)`:
  - $\le 6\text{ hours}$: `fresh` (active current evidence).
  - $6\text{ to }24\text{ hours}$: `stale` (recent historical context; excluded from real-time dynamic elevation).
  - $> 24\text{ hours}$ or unparseable: `expired` (archival only).

### 3.5 Evidence Semantics in UI Legend
- Upgraded [Legend.tsx](file:///c:/Users/rspma/Downloads/NEXSTORM/src/components/map/Legend.tsx) with a dedicated "NEXSTORM Evidence Semantics" section explicitly defining the distinct meaning of Susceptibility, Rainfall-Triggered Risk, Building Exposure, Road Corridors & Routing, Community Reports, and Unknown States.

## 5. Hackathon Demonstration Readiness

### 5.1 What Is Safe to Demonstrate as Verified Capabilities
1. **Interactive Multi-Scale Risk Map**: 250m Amritsar grid (1,998 cells) and 50m GNDU campus subgrid (699 cells) evaluating real-time, replay, and scenario rainfall.
2. **Cell-Level Topographic Explanations**: Inspecting any cell reveals exact factor contributions ($s = \sum v_i \cdot w_i$), terrain flatness overrides, and nearest historical hotspots with news archive links.
3. **Building Catchment Exposure**: Visualizing and clicking real Google Open Buildings footprints around hotspots and campus with verified metric areas ($m^2$) and catchment tiers.
4. **Flood-Aware O/D Route Planning**: Selecting origin and destination pairs across Amritsar (e.g., Mall Road to SSSS Chowk underpass) produces 3 distinct candidates demonstrating underpass avoidance, real OSM road geometry, and travel time estimates at 30 km/h baseline.
5. **Community Observation Reporting**: Submitting citizen reports with client validation, rate limiting, and spatial matching to nearest cell and road.

### 5.2 What Must Be Transparently Presented as Experimental / Unmodeled
1. **Unmodeled Indoor Flood Depth**: Footprint exposure measures terrain macro-catchment overlap; it does not model plinth height or predict indoor damage.
2. **Uncalibrated Baseline Travel Time**: Travel times use an assumed 30 km/h velocity; live traffic congestion, traffic signals, and turn restrictions are unmodeled.
3. **Bidirectional Road Edges**: Legal one-way rules and turn restriction relations are unmodeled due to source GeoPackage limitations.
4. **Corroboration vs Verification**: Corroborated reports reflect multiple matching citizen reports; they do not substitute for official municipal verification.
5. **No Underground Hydraulics**: Static susceptibility captures surface accumulation potential; subsurface storm sewer pipe capacity is unmodeled.
