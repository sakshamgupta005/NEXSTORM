## 2. Fast Repository Audit & Connectivity Resolution

### 2.1 Audit Findings on P11 Graph
The audit revealed why initial pathfinding failed on `public/data/cities/amritsar/roads/road_graph.json`:
- The P11 preparation script registered nodes only at the extreme ends of each OSM `LineString`.
- In urban road networks, side streets and intersections frequently terminate at intermediate vertices of arterial roads (T-junctions). Without noding, edges were not topologically connected at intersections, creating 11,408 isolated components with the largest containing only 458 nodes.

### 2.2 Planar Noding Resolution
- Executed planar geometry noding using `shapely.node` on the 14,853 road geometries.
- Generated `public/data/cities/amritsar/roads/routing_graph.json` (5.97 MB) comprising:
  - **28,418 unique vertices** (snapped within $1\text{ m}$ tolerance).
  - **37,924 connected road edges**.
  - **99.6%** connectivity in the primary connected component.
- Spatial index mapping (`shapely.STRtree`) mapped 100% of noded subsegments back to their parent OSM `segmentId` to preserve multi-cell susceptibility values and underpass flags.

## 3. Flood-Aware Routing Engine & Scoring Mechanics

### 3.1 Edge Weight Functions

| Routing Mode | Weight Formula ($W_e$) | Behavioral Intent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shortest Route** | $W_e = L_e$ (metric length) | Direct shortest path through street network. |
| **Lower Modeled Exposure** | $W_e = L_e \cdot (1 + 4.0 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{s \ge 0.66} + 2.0 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{s \ge 0.33}) + 5000 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{\text{underpass}}$ | Maximum diversion around high-susceptibility zones and structural underpasses. |
| **Balanced Route** | $W_e = L_e \cdot (1 + 1.8 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{s \ge 0.66} + 0.4 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{s \ge 0.33}) + 1000 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{\text{underpass}}$ | Pragmatic trade-off minimizing major flood bottlenecks while preventing excessive detours. |

### 3.2 Calibrated Composite Penalty Score
To avoid double-counting while penalizing unmapped segments, the formula in `RoadRiskService.evaluateCandidateRoutes` was refined:
$$P_R = \min\left(100, \text{round}\left(50 \cdot E_{\text{high}} + 25 \cdot E_{\text{mod}} + \min(30, 15 \cdot N_{\text{obs}}) + \min(20, 10 \cdot N_{\text{underpass}}) + \min(15, 15 \cdot E_{\text{unknown}})\right)\right)$$

Where:
- $E_{\text{high}}, E_{\text{mod}}$: Length-weighted proportion of route in high/moderate susceptibility cells.
- $N_{\text{obs}}$: Number of active, verified or corroborated community waterlogging reports on route.
- $N_{\text{underpass}}$: Number of grade-separated railway/road depression crossings.
- $E_{\text{unknown}}$: Proportion of route in unmapped or unmatched grid cells (missing data is not treated as safe).

## 6. What Is Operational, Experimental, and Blocked

### 6.1 Operational
- **Real Street-Network Routing:** Generates genuine street-following routes on Amritsar's OSM road network; zero straight-line fabrications.
- **Multi-Candidate Generation:** Compares shortest, safest, and balanced routes with real metric lengths and trade-offs.
- **Hazard Penalization:** Reroutes traffic around high-susceptibility macro-catchments and structural underpasses.
- **REST APIs:** Both `POST /api/roads/plan` and `GET /api/roads/plan` operational with parameter validation and error reporting.

### 6.2 Experimental
- **30 km/h Speed Baseline:** Travel times use an uncalibrated 30 km/h baseline velocity without live congestion telemetry.
- **Static Decision Support:** Scores reflect static 250m terrain accumulation potential and reported incidents; they do not guarantee passable street clearance or dynamic runoff depth.

### 6.3 Blocked by Missing Data
- **Legal One-Way & Turn Restrictions:** The underlying GeoPackage extract does not contain OSM turn restriction relations or one-way tags. Graph edges operate bidirectionally.
- **Dynamic Street Closure Telemetry:** Real-time road closures require municipal traffic feeds or live road sensor integrations.
