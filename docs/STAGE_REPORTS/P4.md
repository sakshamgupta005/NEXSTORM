- **Location:** Amritsar city centroid ($31.634^{\circ}\text{N}, 74.872^{\circ}\text{E}$).
- **Period of Record:** 15 monsoon seasons (June 1 – September 30, 2010 through 2024).
- **Sample Count:** 43,920 hourly values (100% complete, 0 missing hours).
- **Computed Thresholds:**
  - **$T_{1\text{h}}$ (99th percentile hourly):** **$3.5\text{ mm}$** (Monsoon hourly maximum: $19.8\text{ mm}$)
  - **$T_{3\text{h}}$ (99th percentile trailing 3h):** **$8.9\text{ mm}$** (Monsoon 3-hour maximum: $45.9\text{ mm}$)
  - **$T_{24\text{h}}$ (IMD Heavy Rain Category):** **$64.5\text{ mm}$** (Fixed standard lower bound)
  - **$T_{72\text{h}}$ (95th percentile trailing 72h):** **$44.8\text{ mm}$** (Monsoon 72-hour maximum: $187.2\text{ mm}$)

- **Limitation Note:**
