## 1. Executive Summary

P29 integrates geolocated image evidence into NEXSTORM's existing building-risk and road/route-risk infrastructure. Rather than creating a detached "image risk" silo, standalone score, or new map, this integration feeds visual proof directly into the established risk calculations:
- **Road Network Risk:** Active verified flooded imagery elevates road segments to `high` risk and applies impedance penalties to candidate evacuation routes.
- **Building Exposure:** Active ground photos confirming water accumulation elevate building `dynamicExposureCategory` to `high_catchment`.
- **Temporal Stratification:** Preserves strict scientific distinction between historical street surveys (e.g., Mapillary baseline passes) and active storm event evidence (< 24h). Historical imagery never triggers active flood alarms.
- **Data Provenance & Integrity:** Preserves source, timestamp, spatial accuracy, attribution, license, and SHA-256 content hashes for deduplication.

## 2. Calculation Trace: Where Danger Scores are Computed

### A. Road Segment Danger & Route Penalty
- **Source File:** `src/lib/roads/service.ts`
- **Method:** `evaluateSegmentRisk(segment, observations)`
  - **Base modeled risk:** Calculated from exact multi-cell intersection with 250m susceptibility grid (`segment.modeledRiskClass`).
  - **Active Image Evidence Integration:** Queries `imageEvidenceService.getRoadImageSummary(segment.segmentId, false)`. Filters for active observations (`!img.isHistorical`).
  - **Elevation Rule:** If active verified flooded image evidence (`floodedRoad: true` or `passability === 'impassable'`) or active verified citizen incident exists, `dynamicRiskClass` elevates to `high`. Active unverified reports elevate `low` to `moderate`.
- **Method:** `evaluateCandidateRoutes(candidates, cityId)`
  - Scans resolved route segments for `hasVerifiedFloodedImage`.
  - Applies a visual flood penalty: `Math.min(30, visuallyConfirmedFloodedOnRoute * 25)`.
  - If any segment has active visually confirmed floodwater, route recommendation transitions to `avoid` and the explanation details `${count} visually confirmed flooded road segment(s)`.

### B. Building Exposure Danger
- **Source File:** `src/lib/buildings/service.ts`
- **Method:** `evaluateFootprintExposure` and `queryBuildings`
  - **Base modeled category:** Derived from spatial footprint intersection with 250m grid cells (`maxCellSusceptibility >= 0.66` -> `high_catchment`, `>= 0.33` -> `moderate_catchment`, else `low_catchment`).
  - **Image Evidence Integration:** Ingests active image evidence attached to the building footprint (`buildingId`) or its primary grid cell (`primaryCellId`).
  - **Dynamic Exposure Elevation:** If active verified image evidence detects flooding (`floodedBuilding: true` or `waterDetected: true`), `imageConfirmedFlooding` is flagged and `dynamicExposureCategory` elevates to `high_catchment`.

## 4. Adaptation of Research Codebases

### A. FloodNet (`sahilkhose/FloodNet` & `BinaLab/FloodNet-Supervised_v1.0`)
- **Adapted Component:** `FloodImageAnalyzer` in `src/lib/images/analyzer.ts`.
- **Classification Taxonomy:** Hierarchical scene understanding identifying:
  - `waterDetected`: boolean
  - `floodedRoad`: boolean
  - `floodedBuilding`: boolean
  - `estimatedWaterDepth`: `'none' | 'curb_ponding' | 'ankle_deep' | 'knee_or_higher' | 'impassable'`
  - `passability`: `'passable' | 'caution' | 'impassable'`
  - `confidence`: calibrated confidence score [0.0, 1.0].

### B. FloodRescue-AI (`aayusPrasad/FloodRescue-AI`)
- **Adapted Component:** Road network risk impedance and evacuation route avoidance in `src/lib/roads/service.ts`.
- **Mechanism:** Translates visual waterlogging severity into road segment danger classes and route composite penalty points.
