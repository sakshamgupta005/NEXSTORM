### Deterministic Partitioning Rule
- Cells are generated across the bounding box in UTM Zone 43N coordinates on a regular grid aligned to integer multiples of cell size (250m for city, 50m for campus).
- Intersection rule: A cell is included if its geometry intersects the boundary with an area overlap $\ge 20\%$ of cell area, or if its centroid falls within the boundary polygon.
- Stable IDs (`A-0001` .. `A-1998` and `G-0001` .. `G-0699`) are assigned deterministically sorted by $Y$ descending (north to south) and $X$ ascending (west to east).

### 5.2 Local Depression Analysis for Model Viability
The local depression metric exhibits a standard deviation of **1.745 m** with an empirical 5th-to-95th percentile spread of **5.385 m** (ranging from $-2.332\text{ m}$ to $+3.053\text{ m}$), with localized basins down to $-9.200\text{ m}$.
**Conclusion for Stage P4:** The terrain variation is sufficiently pronounced across Amritsar's plains to carry meaningful weight in the susceptibility model, particularly when distinguishing low-lying bowls and railway underpasses from surrounding micro-ridges.

### 5.3 Underpass Proxy Rule
Underpass proxies are flagged when:
1. Mapped road attribute explicitly contains `tunnel=yes`, `tunnel=building_passage`, or `layer < 0`; OR
2. Road is categorized as a bridge (`bridge=yes` or `layer > 0`) and has another road polygon/line crossing underneath within 100 metres horizontal distance.
- Detected: 241 cells (12.06% of city grid), clustering along the northern railway tracks and Grand Trunk road intersections.

## 7. Known Limitations & Handoff to Stage P4

1. **Building Footprint Density:** OpenStreetMap building footprint completeness in Amritsar is partial (mean building density is 3.8%). Land cover `builtup_fraction` (mean 54%) from ESA WorldCover provides a far more complete proxy of impervious surface area.
2. **Waterway Drainage Connectivity:** Mapped OSM waterways are scarce (9 features mapped); they do not reflect underground storm drain pipe networks.
3. **No Susceptibility Scoring:** In compliance with Stage P3 scope, susceptibility scores, feature weights, hotspot detection, and rainfall thresholding have been deferred to Stage P4.
4. **Git Tagging:** As required, `p3-data` tag has not been placed. Stage P4 will continue directly on `feat/pipeline`.
