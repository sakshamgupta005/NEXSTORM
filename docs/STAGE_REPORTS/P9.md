# Stage Report P9: Amritsar Building Data Ingestion & Spatial Dataset Pipeline

## 1. Executive Summary

Stage P9 establishes a tested, reproducible geospatial ingestion and preprocessing pipeline for **NEXSTORM**, directly resolving the two foundational data gaps identified in Stage P8:
1. **The Building Sparsity Gap**: Ingested and validated **360,252 real building footprints** from Google Open Buildings V3 across the Amritsar municipal extent (replacing the previous 452 sparse OSM footprints), computing metric footprint areas in UTM Zone 43N, mapping centroids to the 250m susceptibility grid, and generating scalable client-side artifacts.
2. **The Road Topology Gap**: Processed **14,853 OpenStreetMap road segments** covering 1,857.73 km, tagged 5 railway underpasses and 160 bridges, extracted 26,155 topological graph nodes (including 664 junction nodes), and mapped segments to susceptibility grid cells.

All additions strictly preserve the existing area-level risk assessment and hydrometeorological thresholds, enforce scientific non-inference boundaries (no fake building heights or indoor flood depths), and provide deterministic, idempotent offline execution.

## 3. Empirical Ingestion & Preprocessing Findings

### 3.1 Building Footprint Ingestion Audit
- **Source**: Google Open Buildings V3, S2 Level 4 Tile `391`.
- **Bounding Box**: $[74.800^\circ\text{ E}, 31.575^\circ\text{ N}, 74.935^\circ\text{ E}, 31.685^\circ\text{ N}]$.
- **Total Features Retrieved**: **360,252 building polygons**.
- **Valid Geometries Retained**: **360,252 (100.0%)**.
- **Deduplication**: 0 duplicates detected within the tile.
- **Matched to 250m Susceptibility Grid**: **334,476 buildings (92.8%)** fell inside the 1,998-cell municipal grid.
- **Unmatched Outside Grid**: **25,776 buildings (7.2%)** in peri-urban fringes outside the municipal border were truthfully flagged as `unmatched` rather than falsely assigned.
- **GNDU Campus Footprints**: **1,337 real buildings** identified inside the campus boundary (replacing the previous 33 sparse OSM buildings).
- **Hotspot Cluster Footprints**: **17,961 real buildings** identified within 300m of the 15 documented historical waterlogging hotspots.
- **Cells with Buildings**: **1,893 cells** out of 1,998 cells ($94.7\%$) contain one or more structures.

### 3.2 Road Network Preparation Audit
- **Source**: OpenStreetMap contributors (`pipeline/inputs/osm_features.gpkg`).
- **Total Input Segments**: 14,855.
- **Valid Prepared Segments**: 14,853 (2 degenerate segments $<1.0\text{ m}$ dropped).
- **Total Physical Road Length**: **1,857.73 kilometers**.
- **Topological Nodes**: **26,155 vertices** extracted with $10^{-5}$ coordinate rounding ($\approx 1.1\text{m}$ precision).
- **Junction Nodes ($\text{degree} \ge 3$)**: **664 major junctions**.
- **Dead-End Nodes ($\text{degree} = 1$)**: **23,355 terminal spurs**.
- **Identified Underpasses**: **5 segments** explicitly tagged (`tunnel: yes` or `layer: -1`).
- **Identified Bridges / Flyovers**: **160 segments** explicitly tagged (`bridge: yes` or `layer: 1`).
