## 1. Executive Summary & Mission Scope

The primary objective of **P25** is to eliminate the analytical coordinate-decay proxies introduced during the initial Jalandhar prototype bootstrap (P23) and replace them with genuine physical geospatial datasets. In parallel, P25 reconstructs a defensible, traceable historical incident inventory for Jalandhar from accessible archival news reports (2022–2024), establishes an objective city-specific evaluation workflow without premature calibration or synthetic metrics, and enforces strict fail-closed readiness safety gates.

### Core Scientific & Operational Verdicts:
1. **Physical Geospatial Datasets Ingested & Verified:**
   - **Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (30m):** Retrieved directly from the Open Data AWS S3 Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF (COG) bucket (`Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N31_00_E075_00_DEM.tif`). Cropped to Jalandhar buffered bounds and saved to `public/data/cities/jalandhar/rasters/dem.tif` (1.3 MB, $648 \times 504$ pixels, elevation range: $227.3\,\text{m}$ to $258.3\,\text{m}$ MSL).
   - **ESA WorldCover 2021 v200 (10m):** Retrieved directly from the Open Data AWS S3 COG bucket (`ESA_WorldCover_10m_2021_v200_N30E075_Map.tif`). Cropped to Jalandhar buffered bounds and saved to `public/data/cities/jalandhar/rasters/worldcover.tif` (3.6 MB, $2,160 \times 1,680$ pixels, 34.5% Built-up Class 50 pixels).
   - **OpenStreetMap Vectors:** Ingested 17,358 real highway and waterway segments via Overpass API into `public/data/cities/jalandhar/osm_features.gpkg`. Sparse building footprint density was explicitly supplemented with 10m ESA WorldCover built-up pixels rather than assuming missing buildings equal low density.
2. **Analytical Proxies Completely Replaced:**
   - Coordinate distance-decay formulas (`np.exp(-3.5 * dist_to_center)`) and synthetic linear slope gradients (`alluvial_slope`) were completely purged from feature generation.
   - Replaced by focal neighborhood terrain position index (TPI, 750m window), physical 2D slope gradient, flat plain thresholding ($< 0.5^\circ$), pixel-derived built-up fractions, and real road densities.
3. **Severe Mid-Band Compression Eliminated:**
   - Susceptibility standard deviation expanded from $\sigma = 0.0828$ (compressed proxy) to $\sigma = 0.1505$ (physical terrain).
   - Mid-band concentration dropped from $93.16\%$ down to $66.48\%$, with healthy low ($24.51\%$) and high ($9.00\%$) susceptibility distribution.
   - Low-point artifact collapsed from $64.71\%$ down to $12.45\%$, matching genuine geomorphological depressions.
4. **Historical Incident Inventory Reconstructed:**
   - Reconstructed 10 traceable incident records from *The Tribune* archives (2022–2024).
   - 1 record excluded with documented rationale (`EXCL-JAL-2023-08-05-EFFLUENT-SPILL`, industrial effluent discharge without rain).
   - 9 valid waterlogging incidents deduplicated into exactly 5 independent storm events across 6 documented priority choke points (Damoria Railway Underbridge, 120 Feet Road / Basti Guzan, Ladowali Road, Preet Nagar underpass, Football Chowk, Burlton Park).
5. **Defensible Evaluation Tooling Delivered:**
   - Built `pipeline/scripts/18_evaluate_jalandhar.py`. Spatially matched all 9 incidents to analysis grid cells (`J-1048`, `J-1411`, `J-1429`, `J-0842`, `J-1259`, `J-0832`).
   - Achieved 100% incident detection ($S \ge 0.33$) and 100% storm detection.
   - Strictly withheld False Alarm Rate (FAR), Specificity, and ROC-AUC due to unobserved negative space.
6. **Readiness State Machine Enforced:**
   - Jalandhar remains strictly in `readinessState: "evidence_insufficient"` with all external alert dispatch suppressed. Advancing to `evaluation_ready` requires localized sub-daily gauge telemetry or municipal complaint registers, preserving Amritsar as the sole calibrated reference.

## 3. Phase 2 — Replacement of Proxy-Derived Features

Feature extraction was implemented in `pipeline/scripts/compute_jalandhar_physical_features.py`:

1. **Hydrological Topography & D8 Evaluation:**
   - Jalandhar lies on a flat alluvial plain with an elevation span of only $31\,\text{m}$ ($227.3\,\text{m}$ to $258.3\,\text{m}$ MSL) over $147\,\text{km}^2$.
   - **Hydrological Routing Decision:** D8 flow accumulation algorithms on flat terrain create severe parallel striping artifacts unless extensive artificial trenching/stream burning is performed.
   - **Adopted Solution:** Replaced D8 accumulation with a multi-scale **Topographic Position Index (TPI)** using a $750\,\text{m}$ focal neighborhood ($25 \times 25$ DEM pixels). Depressions where elevation is below the neighborhood mean ($\text{elev} < \mu_{750\text{m}}$) represent genuine physical water collection pockets.
2. **Metric Slope Calculation:**
   - 2D gradient calculated using metric pixel spacing ($\Delta x \approx 26.3\,\text{m}$, $\Delta y \approx 30.8\,\text{m}$), yielding true physical slope in degrees.
   - `f_flatness` computed as $\exp(-\text{slope} / 0.5^\circ)$, accurately capturing flat flood-prone zones.
3. **Built-Up & Urban Density:**
   - Sampled $2,160 \times 1,680$ pixels of ESA WorldCover v200. Evaluated fraction of Built-up pixels (Class 50) within each $250\,\text{m}$ cell.
   - Real OSM road segment lengths were summed per cell and normalized.
4. **Composite Susceptibility:**
   - Weighted combination using validated physical parameters ($w_{\text{elev}}=0.25, w_{\text{slope}}=0.20, w_{\text{built}}=0.25, w_{\text{road}}=0.15, w_{\text{dep}}=0.15$).
