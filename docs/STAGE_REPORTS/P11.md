## 1. Executive Summary

Stage P11 establishes the third foundational pillar of the **NEXSTORM** urban waterlogging intelligence system: **defensible road-segment waterlogging risk assessment and candidate route flood exposure comparison**.

NEXSTORM operates on three equally critical pillars:
1. **Area-level waterlogging susceptibility and rainfall-triggered risk** (250m macro-catchment grid across Amritsar).
2. **Building-level exposure assessment** (individual footprint associations with macro-catchments and ground elevations).
3. **Road-segment risk assessment & flood-aware routing foundation** (corridor risk aggregation, underpass recognition, dynamic observation elevation, and route risk comparison).

Before P11, road geometries in the repository existed merely as raw GIS lines snapped to a single nearest centroid. P11 delivers:
- **Exact Line-in-Polygon Multi-Cell Intersections:** All 14,853 road segments are spatially intersected with the 1,998-cell 250m susceptibility grid using GeoPandas STRtree spatial indexing, calculating exact subsegment metric lengths ($L_{i,c}$) and proportional shares ($\text{fraction}_{i,c}$).
- **Grade-Separated Depression Recognition:** Identifies and explicitly tags 5 railway/road underpasses and 160 elevated bridges/flyovers.
- **Dynamic Ground-Truth Reconcilation:** Dynamically links road segments to P10 verified and corroborated waterlogging observations. Active verified reports elevate corridor risk to `high`, and corroborated reports elevate risk to at least `moderate`.
- **Candidate Route Flood Exposure Comparison:** A dedicated API endpoint (`POST /api/roads/route-compare`) and UI panel evaluate candidate route polylines, computing length-weighted exposure to risk classes, underpass bottlenecks, active incident penalties, and a composite risk penalty score ($0\text{--}100$).
- **Topological Graph Extraction:** Generates a directed graph of 26,155 vertices (664 junctions, 23,355 dead ends).
- **Interactive Visualization & API:** Adds an optional, 60fps major corridor layer (773 arterial/trunk/primary/secondary/tertiary/underpass segments) with detailed inspection popups, paired with a filterable `GET /api/roads/risk` endpoint.

## 4. Road Risk Assessment Engine & Dynamic Scoring

### 4.1 Base Modeled Risk
Static road risk reflects topographic and urban built-up runoff accumulation:
- **High Risk:** $s_{\max} \ge 0.66$ OR `isUnderpass = true`.
- **Moderate Risk:** $0.33 \le s_{\max} < 0.66$.
- **Low Risk:** $s_{\max} < 0.33$.

### 4.2 Dynamic Evidence Elevation
Modeled risk is dynamically combined with P10 observation evidence:
- **Active Verified Waterlogging Incident:** Dynamic risk elevated to `high`.
- **Active Corroborated Reports (Cluster $\ge 2$):** Dynamic risk elevated to at least `moderate`.
- **Tagged Railway Underpass:** Retains physical depression vulnerability (minimum `moderate`, base `high`).
- **Expired / Rejected Reports:** Excluded from dynamic hazard calculations.

## 5. Candidate Route Flood Exposure Comparison

The route risk evaluation engine (`POST /api/roads/route-compare` and `RoadRiskService.evaluateCandidateRoutes`) accepts candidate route polylines and computes:
- **Length-Weighted Exposure by Risk Class:**
  $$E_{\text{high}} = \frac{\sum_{s \in S_{\text{high}}} L_s}{L_{\text{total}}}, \quad E_{\text{moderate}} = \frac{\sum_{s \in S_{\text{mod}}} L_s}{L_{\text{total}}}, \quad E_{\text{low}} = 1.0 - E_{\text{high}} - E_{\text{moderate}}$$
- **Composite Risk Penalty Score ($0\text{--}100$):**
  $$P_R = \min\left(100, \text{round}\left(50 \cdot E_{\text{high}} + 25 \cdot E_{\text{moderate}} + \min(30, 15 \cdot N_{\text{obs}}) + \min(20, 10 \cdot N_{\text{underpass}})\right)\right)$$
- **Safety Recommendations:**
  - `recommended`: $P_R < 20$, no underpasses.
  - `caution`: $20 \le P_R < 50$.
  - `avoid`: $P_R \ge 50$, underpass crossing present, or $E_{\text{high}} > 0.30$.

## 8. What Is Operational, Experimental, and Blocked

### 8.1 Fully Operational
- **14,853 Road Segments with Multi-Cell Intersections:** Metric lengths, cell IDs, and length fractions calculated and indexed.
- **Underpass Tagging:** 5 railway/road underpasses identified and assigned heightened baseline vulnerability.
- **Dynamic Observation Linking:** Verified and corroborated reports dynamically elevate road segment risk.
- **Route Risk Scoring:** `POST /api/roads/route-compare` accepts candidate routes and returns auditable flood exposure comparisons and safety recommendations.
- **REST API:** `GET /api/roads/risk` supports viewport bounding boxes, road classes, underpasses, and pagination.
- **Console Map Layer & Panel:** 773 major corridors rendered with risk-aware styling and interactive route comparison tool.

### 8.2 Experimental
- **Coordinate-to-Segment Snapping for Arbitrary Polylines:** When candidate routes do not provide explicit segment IDs, routes snap to the nearest road segments within $0.015^\circ$ ($\approx 1.5\text{ km}$).
- **Travel Time Estimation:** Derived using an assumed urban baseline velocity ($30\text{ km/h}$ / $8.33\text{ m/s}$) rather than live traffic telemetry.

### 8.3 Blocked by Missing Data
- **Turn-Restriction & Legal One-Way Enforcement:** The source `osm_features.gpkg` does not include `oneway` tags or relation XML turn restrictions. Edges in the current graph are bidirectional. Enforcing legal one-ways requires ingesting raw OSM PBF data in Stage P13.
- **Real-Time Street Clearance:** Predictions indicate runoff accumulation potential; they do not guarantee passage or street clearance depth without field gauges.

## 9. Recommendations for Stage P12

Stage P12 should focus on **Building-Level Exposure Assessment**, advancing the second pillar of NEXSTORM:
1. **Preserve Scientific Boundaries:** Keep area-level susceptibility ($S$), building exposure (spatial overlap with hazardous zones and terrain elevation), and building structural vulnerability (construction material, plinth height, number of stories) strictly distinct.
2. **Ingest Amritsar Building Stock:** Scale beyond the preliminary 452 OSM buildings to ingest comprehensive Amritsar building footprints (from Open Buildings v3 or municipal GIS).
3. **Terrain Elevation Differential:** Compute relative plinth risk by calculating building ground elevation relative to the surrounding 250m cell minimum elevation ($\Delta z = z_{\text{building}} - z_{\text{cell, min}}$).
4. **Building Risk API & Visualization:** Provide building-level exposure inspection in the console without degrading map render performance (vector tile or viewport-bounded loading).
