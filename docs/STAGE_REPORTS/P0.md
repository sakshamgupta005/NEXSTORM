## 3. Cleanup Waves & Justifications

### Wave 1: Payments & Webhook Toolkit
- **Justification:** E-commerce payments, Stripe webhooks, and billing idempotency are completely unrelated to meteorological heavy rainfall forecasting and urban waterlogging prediction.
- **Evidence:** Grep search proved zero inbound runtime imports in Next.js App Router routes or UI components. Only referenced in its own test suite and documentation.
- **Verification:** Typecheck passed; 65 test files and 661 tests passed (18 tests removed).
- **Commit:** `f17ca95 chore(p0): wave 1 - remove unrelated payments toolkit`

### Wave 2: Speech & Voice Toolkit
- **Justification:** Audio recording, Whisper transcription (STT), and text-to-speech (TTS) are irrelevant to hydrological modeling, spatial rain grids, and flood risk alerts.
- **Evidence:** Only imported by `use-audio-recorder.ts`, its unit test, and component test.
- **Verification:** Typecheck passed; 63 test files and 639 tests passed (22 tests removed).
- **Commit:** `0b78ee5 chore(p0): wave 2 - remove unrelated speech and audio toolkit`

### Wave 3: Computer Vision & Camera / OCR Toolkit
- **Justification:** Camera preview, video frame inspection, and OCR document extraction have zero application to geospatial precipitation grids or urban flood routing.
- **Evidence:** Only imported by `camera-preview.tsx`, `use-camera.ts`, and their dedicated tests.
- **Verification:** Typecheck passed; 60 test files and 608 tests passed (31 tests removed).
- **Commit:** `0e07e83 chore(p0): wave 3 - remove unrelated vision, ocr, and camera toolkit`
