# Amritsar Data Foundation: Spatial Inventory, Feasibility & Integrity Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

This document establishes the empirical data foundation for **NEXSTORM Stage P8**, conducting a rigorous inventory of verified spatial datasets currently present in the repository and evaluating the feasibility, licensing, and scientific limits of acquiring candidate external datasets for Amritsar, Punjab, India.

NEXSTORM's long-term vision requires integrating three equal pillars:
1. **Area-level risk assessment**: macro-catchment waterlogging susceptibility combining terrain, land cover, and multi-window rainfall accumulations.
2. **Building-level exposure assessment**: associating building footprints with hazard layers without making unsupported indoor flooding or structural damage claims.
3. **Road-level risk assessment and flood-aware routing**: associating road corridors with catchment hazard, underpass vulnerability, and verified observations to compare candidate routes.

## 4. Building Dataset Feasibility Audit

### 4.1 Current Repository Status: The OSM Sparsity Gap
Inspection of `pipeline/inputs/osm_features.gpkg` reveals only **452 building polygons** in the entire Amritsar municipal extent:
- 419 polygons have no metadata other than `building: yes`.
- 33 polygons have specific names or tags (e.g., Golden Temple complex, GNDU administrative block, Khalsa College).
- An estimated 300,000+ urban structures exist in Amritsar. OSM building data represents $<0.15\%$ coverage and is heavily biased toward institutional and heritage landmarks.
- **Scientific Conclusion**: In-repository OSM data is insufficient for building-level exposure assessment across the city.

## 5. Road Network & Routing Feasibility Audit

### 5.1 Current Repository Status: OSM Road Geometry
Inspection of `pipeline/inputs/osm_features.gpkg` confirms **14,855 road segments** in WGS84 LineString format:
- Residential: 12,426 segments
- Service roads: 1,128 segments
- Tertiary roads: 236 segments
- Primary roads: 183 segments
- Trunk roads: 152 segments
- Secondary roads: 123 segments
- Unclassified / Track: 24 segments

**The Missing Network Topology Gap**:
While geometric polylines exist, they do **not** form a connected topological routing graph:
1. No explicit intersection nodes or edge connectivity pointers.
2. No one-way traffic directionality or turn restriction tags.
3. No grade separation flags (bridges vs. underpasses vs. at-grade crossings).
4. No speed limits or dynamic traversal weights.

## 6. Historical Incidents & Fresh Community Observations

### 6.1 Historical Incident Baseline (15 Hotspots)
The 15 documented historical hotspots in `public/data/cities/amritsar/hotspots.json` represent ground truth from news records:
- **Spatial Precision**: Point coordinates verified to prominent junctions (e.g., SSSS Chowk Underpass: `31.6480° N, 74.8780° E`).
- **Event Dates**: Spanning 2023 and 2024 monsoon seasons (e.g., 2023-07-09, 2023-08-14, 2024-08-11).
- **Underpass Vulnerability**: 3 hotspots represent railway underpasses (SSSS Chowk, Putlighar Underpass, Batala Road Underpass) where roadway grade drops several meters below ambient alluvial elevation.

### 6.2 Snapping Citizen Observations: Safe Geodetic Matching Rules
When integrating fresh community observations:
1. **Strict Distance Tolerance**: A point observation may only be associated with a road segment if its orthogonal distance $d_{\perp} \le 30\text{ m}$.
2. **Preserve Unmatched Status**: Observations $>30\text{ m}$ from any road centerline must remain `unmatched` with an explicit reason (`no_road_within_tolerance`). Arbitrary snapping to the nearest road is strictly prohibited.
3. **Verification Levels**: Every observation must carry an immutable verification status:
   - `unverified`: Raw citizen submission.
   - `corroborated`: Multiple independent submissions within 100m and 30 minutes.
   - `verified`: Ground-truth confirmation by municipal personnel or police advisory.

## 8. Summary of Data Integrity Principles

1. **No Synthetic Extrapolation**: Missing data is reported truthfully as missing or unavailable.
2. **Clear Spatial Separation**: Macro-catchment grid cells, building footprints, and road centerlines must not conflate their spatial meanings.
3. **No False Certainty**: Road corridor susceptibility does not guarantee that a road is blocked; building exposure does not predict indoor flood depth.
